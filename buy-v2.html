On Aruba

Plaza Bookshop, Oranjestad

Ask in store

On the shelf at Afl. 60. Ave Milio Croes 8A, Oranjestad. Tel 582 1821.

Coming off a cruise ship?

Plaza Bookshop is in downtown Oranjestad, a few minutes from the cruise terminal by taxi. Taxis wait directly outside the terminal building and fares on Aruba are fixed by destination, not metered, so agree the price before you set off.

"Ave Milio Croes 8A, near the Plaza. I am going to Plaza Bookshop."

If the shop has sold out, they can tell you whether De Wit & Van Dorp still has copies before you make a second trip.
